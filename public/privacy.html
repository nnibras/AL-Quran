Privacy Policy

Al-Quran app · Last updated: 26 November 2026

Al-Quran lets you read, search and listen to the Quran. It is built to work without collecting any information about you.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves the following only on your own device. It is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Your settings: theme, translation, reciter, Arabic text size and reading view.
  • Your bookmarks and the last surah or juz you read.

You can erase this at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Internet access

The Quran text and translations are included in the app. The internet is used only to stream recitation audio when you press play. Audio is loaded from these third-party services:

  • Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network)
  • GitHub (github.com), for some reciters

Like any website, these services receive standard technical information needed to deliver the audio, such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply to that information. The app does not send them anything else.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
